The studio

Led by Meher Choupane, built on plain talk and tidy drawings.

Arthanisa is a small, Thane-based design practice working across homes, offices, and retail interiors. The studio favours clear communication over jargon — every concept is walked through in detail before anything goes to execution, so clients always know what they're approving and why.

Projects stay dependable rather than dramatic: honest quotes, realistic timelines, and a design point of view that's tailored to how each client actually lives or works, not a catalogue look dropped into their space.

What we design

Four ways into a project.

01

Residential Interiors

Full-home or single-room design — layout, furniture, lighting, and finishes planned as one coherent scheme.

02

Commercial & Retail

Offices, clinics, and storefronts designed to work for staff and customers, not just look good in photos.

How a project runs

Four stages, no shortcuts.

01

Site visit & brief

We measure the space and listen to how you actually want to use it — budget and timeline get set here too.

02

Concept & layout

Mood direction, material palette, and a first layout — reviewed together before anything is finalised.

03

Detailed drawings

Working drawings, 3D views, and a firm quote, so there are no surprises once work begins on site.

04

Execution & handover

The studio supervises the build through to snag-free handover, with sign-off at every major milestone.
